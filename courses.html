Term-time courses

Robotics and STEM Courses

Weekly robotics and STEM courses at Edinburgh Central Mosque, in two age groups. Each group runs one module per school term.

Age groups

Junior and Senior

Each group has one module per school term, with one session a week. Sessions start at 60 minutes and may be extended later if that suits students and mentors. The outlines below describe the shape of the year. Pacing will vary by group, so some cohorts will move faster or slower through a term than others.

Ages 8 to 12

Junior

The transition from block-based coding into real programming, alongside first hands-on electronics and design work.

  • Term 1
    From Blocks to Python

    Moving from visual block coding into real Python, building toward a small game or program students design and demonstrate themselves.

  • Term 2
    Circuits and Sensors

    Hands-on electronics: wiring circuits, reading sensors, and controlling motors, building toward a small working invention that senses and responds to the world around it.

  • Term 3
    Design and Build

    An introduction to 3D design, creating real objects on screen and seeing a selection brought to life on Bunyaan's 3D printer.

Ages 13 and over

Senior

Deeper programming, physical computing projects, and one of two advanced specialisms in the final term.

  • Term 1
    Python Programming

    Structured programming with a real project of their own, with an emphasis on good habits, clear code, and problem-solving.

  • Term 2
    Raspberry Pi and Smart Devices

    Building working devices with real sensors and microcontrollers, culminating in a small team-built invention.

  • Term 3
    One of two specialisms

    Bunyaan runs one of two specialisms each year: CAD and 3D Design (advanced modelling and functional prototyping) or AI and ML (a hands-on introduction to how machine learning models are trained and used).
